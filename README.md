# 🏛️ Pharaohs Legacy – Explore the Wonders of Egypt

**Pharaohs Legacy** is a comprehensive Egyptian tourism web application designed to promote Egypt as a global travel destination by showcasing its rich history, ancient civilization, cultural heritage, and modern attractions.

The platform provides tourists from around the world with an interactive and informative experience, helping them discover Egypt's most iconic landmarks, explore historical sites, find hotels and restaurants, plan trips, and experience Egyptian attractions through immersive virtual tours.

By combining tourism information, travel services, interactive features, and modern web technologies, Pharaohs Legacy aims to make discovering and exploring Egypt easier, more accessible, and more engaging.

## 🌍 Project Overview

Egypt is home to one of the world's oldest and most fascinating civilizations, with thousands of years of history, remarkable archaeological sites, religious landmarks, beautiful landscapes, and vibrant modern cities.

Pharaohs Legacy brings these experiences together in one digital platform.

The application is designed to serve as a tourism guide where visitors can:

- Discover Egypt's historical, religious, and modern landmarks.
- Learn about Egyptian civilization and cultural heritage.
- Explore hotels, restaurants, airports, and shopping destinations.
- Browse and book organized trips within Egypt.
- Experience selected attractions through 360-degree virtual tours.
- Share travel experiences and stories with other visitors.
- Save favorite destinations for future reference.
- Manage their personal profiles and travel-related information.

## ✨ Main Features

### 1. 🏛️ Landmarks & Historical Sites

Explore Egypt's remarkable landmarks through organized categories:

- **Pharaonic Landmarks:** Discover ancient Egyptian temples, pyramids, tombs, museums, and archaeological sites.
- **Islamic Landmarks:** Explore historic mosques, Islamic architecture, and cultural monuments.
- **Coptic Landmarks:** Discover churches, monasteries, and important Christian heritage sites.
- **Modern Landmarks:** Explore contemporary attractions and notable destinations across Egypt.

Each landmark provides visitors with useful information to help them understand its historical significance and plan their visit.

### 2. 🏨 Hotels

A dedicated section for discovering accommodation options across Egypt.

Features include:
- Hotel listings with detailed information.
- Hotel cards displaying essential details.
- Filtering options to help visitors find suitable accommodation.
- Information to support travel and accommodation planning.

### 3. 🧭 Trips & Travel Experiences

Discover organized trips and travel experiences within Egypt.

Features include:
- Browse available trips.
- Explore trip details and destinations.
- Discover travel experiences across different Egyptian locations.
- Book trips through an integrated booking workflow.
- Visa card payment integration.

### 4. 🍽️ Restaurants

Discover restaurants and dining destinations throughout Egypt.

The restaurants section helps tourists explore local dining options, discover Egyptian cuisine, and find places to enjoy different food experiences.

### 5. ✈️ Airports

Provides information about airports in Egypt to help tourists understand available arrival and departure destinations and navigate their travel plans more easily.

### 6. 🛍️ Shopping Centers

Discover shopping destinations and commercial centers across Egypt, helping tourists explore shopping opportunities during their visits.

### 7. 🌐 Virtual Tours (360°)

One of the platform's immersive features.

Visitors can explore selected Egyptian attractions through 360-degree virtual tours, allowing them to experience destinations digitally before visiting them in person.

This feature aims to make Egyptian tourism more interactive and accessible to international visitors.

### 8. 📝 Stories & Travel Experiences

A community-oriented section where registered users can share their travel experiences and impressions.

Features include:
- Publish personal travel stories.
- Share descriptions and images.
- Browse stories shared by other visitors.
- Discover destinations through real visitor experiences.

### 9. ❤️ Favorites

Users can save their preferred landmarks and destinations for easier access later.

This feature helps visitors organize the places they are interested in exploring.

### 10. 👤 User Profile & Authentication

A personal account system that allows users to access personalized platform features.

Features include:
- User registration and login.
- Google authentication.
- Personal profile management.
- Profile information and image management.
- Access to user-related content and saved favorites.

## 🏗️ Application Architecture

Pharaohs Legacy uses a frontend-backend architecture.

**Frontend:** A responsive, multi-page web application built using HTML, CSS, and Vanilla JavaScript. It communicates with the backend through API requests.

**Backend:** A Laravel-based REST API responsible for business logic, authentication, data management, and application services.

**Database:** Stores application data, including users, landmarks, hotels, trips, restaurants, stories, and other platform-related information.

**API Communication:** The frontend consumes backend endpoints to retrieve and manage application data dynamically.

**Deployment:** The frontend is deployed through Cloudflare Pages, while the backend API is hosted separately.
